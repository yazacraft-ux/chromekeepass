FAQ

Good to know.

Is the Chrome extension available now?+

Not yet. The new ChromeKeePass extension is currently in preparation. The download button will appear here when it is published.

Is ChromeKeePass affiliated with Google?+

No. ChromeKeePass is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Will ChromeKeePass replace KeePass?+

No. The goal is to provide a convenient browser companion for a KeePass-based workflow.
