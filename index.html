FAQ

Good to know.

Can I install ChromeKeePass now?+

Yes. ChromeKeePass is available free on the Chrome Web Store. Use any “Add to Chrome” button on this site to open the official listing.

Does ChromeKeePass work without KeePass?+

Yes. The secure password generator and signup password suggestions work without a KeePass connection.

How does KeePass integration work?+

ChromeKeePass connects to a KeePassHttp service running locally on your computer. Once paired, it can retrieve matching credentials for the current website and fill them when you choose an entry.

Does ChromeKeePass send my passwords to a server?+

ChromeKeePass does not operate a cloud password vault. Generated passwords are created locally, and KeePass communication is with the KeePassHttp service on your own computer.

Is ChromeKeePass affiliated with Google?+

No. ChromeKeePass is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
